1. Cricket Ka Itihas - Kaha Se Shuru Hua?

Cricket ka itihas lagbhag 500 saal purana hai. Ye khel England se shuru hua tha. 16vi sadi me England ke bachche ek lathi aur ek gend se khelte the, jisko baad me cricket kaha gaya. Dheere dheere ye khel England ke bade logo ka pasandida khel ban gaya. 1700 ke aas paas iske pehle niyam banaye gaye. Tab cricket me sirf 2 stump hote the, baad me 3 stump kiye gaye. England ne jab duniya ke alag alag desho pe raaj kiya, toh wo apne saath cricket ko bhi le gaya. Isi tarah cricket Australia, India, Pakistan, West Indies, South Africa, New Zealand, Sri Lanka aur Bangladesh tak pahunch gaya.

India me cricket 1721 me aaya jab British East India Company ke log yaha khelte the. Pehle toh sirf angrez hi khelte the, par dheere dheere Indian log bhi isko seekhne lage. 1848 me Mumbai me pehla Indian cricket club bana jiska naam tha Oriental Cricket Club. Uske baad 1933 me India ne apna pehla Test match England ke khilaf khela. Tab se lekar aaj tak India cricket ka sabse bada desh ban gaya hai. Aaj IPL duniya ki sabse badi cricket league hai aur India me cricket ko dekhne wale 100 crore se zyada log hai.

Cricket ke itihas me kai bade badlav aaye. Pehle Test cricket 5 din ka hota tha, phir 1971 me One Day cricket aaya jo 1 din me khatam hota tha. Fir 2003 me T20 aaya jo sirf 3 ghante me khatam hota hai. Har format ne cricket ko aur zyada popular banaya. Aaj cricket sirf England ka khel nahi hai, ye Asia ka sabse bada khel hai. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh aur Sri Lanka me cricket ko log dil se chahte hai. Gully cricket se lekar World Cup tak, har jagah cricket hi cricket hai.

Ek zamana tha jab cricket sirf ameer logo ka khel mana jata tha, jo safed kapde pehen ke khelte the. Par aaj cricket har gali, har mohalle, har gaon me khela jata hai. Bachcha ho ya budha, sabko cricket pasand hai. Jab India Pakistan ka match hota hai, toh pura desh ruk jata hai. TV, mobile sab pe sirf cricket hi dikhta hai. Isiliye kehte hai ki cricket ek khel nahi, ek emotion hai.

2. Cricket Ke Basic Rules - Aasan Bhasha Me

Cricket me 2 team hoti hai, har team me 11 players hote hai. Ek team pehle batting karti hai aur dusri bowling aur fielding. Batting wali team run banati hai aur bowling wali team unko out karne ki koshish karti hai. Match ke end me jiske zyada run hote hai, wo jeet jati hai. Ye sunne me bahut aasan lagta hai, par iske andar bahut sare niyam hai.

Pitch 22 yard lambi hoti hai, jiske dono taraf 3-3 stump lage hote hai. Bowler ek taraf se gend phenkta hai aur batsman dusri taraf se usko bat se marta hai. Agar batsman gend ko maar ke dusri taraf tak bhag jaye toh 1 run milta hai. Agar gend boundary ke bahar jameen ko chhu kar jaye toh 4 run milte hai aur agar bina jameen chhuye seedha bahar jaye toh 6 run milte hai. Isko chauka aur chhakka kehte hai.

Out hone ke kai tarike hai - Bowled, Caught, LBW, Run Out, Stumped, Hit Wicket. Sabse common hai Caught Out, jab batsman ne gend mari aur fielder ne usko hawa me pakad liya. LBW thoda mushkil hai, jab gend batsman ke pair pe lagti hai aur umpire ko lagta hai ki gend stump pe lagti, toh wo out de deta hai. Aaj kal DRS system bhi aa gaya hai, jisme player umpire ke faisle ko challenge kar sakta hai. Third umpire TV dekh ke faisla karta hai.

Over me 6 gend hoti hai. Har over ke baad bowler badal jata hai. Test me koi limit nahi hoti, par ODI me har bowler sirf 10 over daal sakta hai aur T20 me sirf 4 over. Wide ball aur No Ball pe extra run milta hai aur ek extra gend bhi milti hai. No Ball pe batsman out nahi hota, sirf Run Out ho sakta hai. Isko Free Hit bhi kehte hai T20 me.

Umpire match ka sabse bada adhikari hota hai. Ground me 2 umpire hote hai, ek bowler ke peeche aur ek side me. Teesra umpire TV room me hota hai. Aaj kal to Snickometer aur Hawk Eye jaisi technology bhi aa gayi hai, jisse pata chalta hai ki gend bat se lagi ya nahi. Isse cricket aur zyada fair ho gaya hai.

3. Cricket Ke 3 Formats - Test, ODI Aur T20

A. Test Cricket - Asli Cricket

Test cricket cricket ka sabse purana aur sabse mushkil format hai. Ye 5 din tak chalta hai. Har team ko 2-2 baar batting karne ka mauka milta hai. Isme player ki asli pariksha hoti hai - patience, technique aur stamina. Test me jo player acha hai, usko hi asli legend mana jata hai. Sachin Tendulkar ne 200 Test khele, jo aaj tak kisi ne nahi khela. Test me ek din me 90 over hote hai. Lal gend se khela jata hai aur sab safed kapde pehente hai. Test cricket me draw bhi ho sakta hai, agar 5 din me result na aaye toh.

Test cricket me kai yaadgar pal aaye hai. 2001 me India ne Australia ko Kolkata me haraya tha, jab Laxman ne 281 run banaye the aur Harbhajan ne hat-trick li thi. Wo match India ne follow-on ke baad jeeta tha, jo itihas me bahut kam hota hai. Test cricket slow lagta hai, par jo isko samajhta hai, usko isse zyada maza kisi me nahi aata.

B. ODI Cricket - 50 Over Ka Khel

ODI matlab One Day International. Ye 1971 me shuru hua. Isme har team 50 over khelti hai. Pehle 60 over ka hota tha, baad me 50 over ka kar diya gaya. ODI ne cricket ko popular banaya kyuki ye 1 din me khatam ho jata hai. Isme safed gend ki jagah safed gend aur rangeen kapde aaye. World Cup bhi ODI format me hi khela jata hai. 1983 me India ne pehli baar World Cup jeeta tha Kapil Dev ki kaptani me. Us jeet ne India me cricket ko badal diya.

ODI me batting me powerplay hota hai, jisme sirf 2 fielder bahar reh sakte hai. Iska fayda uthake batsman shuru me tez run banata hai. Beech ke over me spin bowler aate hai aur end me fast bowler yorker dalte hai. 2011 me India ne dobara World Cup jeeta Dhoni ki kaptani me. Wo din har Indian kabhi nahi bhoolega jab Dhoni ne chhakka maar ke match jitaya tha.

C. T20 Cricket - 3 Ghante Ka Dhamaka

T20 sabse naya aur sabse tez format hai. Ye 2003 me England me shuru hua aur 2007 me pehla T20 World Cup hua, jisko India ne jeeta. Isme har team sirf 20 over khelti hai, matlab pura match 3 ghante me khatam. Isme har gend pe chauka chhakka marne ki koshish hoti hai. Isi format ki wajah se IPL bana, jo aaj duniya ki sabse ameer league hai.

T20 ne naye shots ko janm diya - Scoop shot, Ramp shot, Switch hit, Helicopter shot. Dhoni ka Helicopter shot toh duniya bhar me mashhoor hai. T20 me bowler ko bhi bahut smart hona padta hai. Slow ball, Yorker, Knuckle ball jaise naye variation aaye. Aaj kal har desh ki apni T20 league hai - IPL, BBL, PSL, CPL. Par IPL sabse bada hai.

4. IPL - Indian Premier League Ki Kahani

IPL 2008 me shuru hua. Lalit Modi ne isko banaya tha. Shuru me logo ne socha ye 2-3 saal me band ho jayega, par aaj IPL duniya ki sabse badi cricket league hai. Isme 10 team hai - Mumbai Indians, Chennai Super Kings, Royal Challengers Bangalore, Kolkata Knight Riders, Delhi Capitals, Punjab Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Lucknow Super Giants, Gujarat Titans.

IPL ne kai naye players ko star banaya. Jasprit Bumrah, Hardik Pandya, Rishabh Pant, Shubman Gill sab IPL se hi aaye. IPL me paisa bhi bahut hai. Ek player ko 25 crore tak milte hai. 2022 me IPL ke media rights 48,000 crore me bike the. Ye duniya me football ke baad dusri sabse mehngi league hai.

CSK aur MI sabse safal team hai, dono ne 5-5 baar trophy jeeti hai. Dhoni aur Rohit Sharma sabse safal kaptan hai. Virat Kohli IPL me sabse zyada run banane wale player hai. Unhone ek season me 973 run banaye the, jo aaj tak koi nahi tod paya. IPL ka final dekhne ke liye log mahino pehle ticket book karte hai. IPL ke time India me shaadi bhi kam hoti hai, kyuki sabko match dekhna hota hai.

IPL ne cricket ko entertainment bana diya. Cheerleaders, Music, Bollywood stars - sab kuch IPL me hai. Shahrukh Khan KKR ke malik hai, Preity Zinta Punjab ki. IPL me har saal naye record bante hai. Ek match me 250 run banna ab aam baat ho gayi hai. 2024 me SRH ne 287 run banaye the, jo IPL ka sabse bada score hai.

5. World Cup - Har 4 Saal Ka Maha Kumbh

Cricket World Cup har 4 saal me hota hai. Pehla World Cup 1975 me England me hua, jisko West Indies ne jeeta. Tab West Indies duniya ki sabse taqatwar team thi. Clive Lloyd ki team ne lagatar 2 World Cup jeete. 1983 me India ne West Indies ko hara ke sabko chauka diya. Kapil Dev ne 175 run ki pari kheli thi Zimbabwe ke khilaf, jab India 17 run pe 5 wicket kho chuka tha. Wo pari aaj bhi legend hai.

1992 me rangeen kapde aur safed gend aayi. 1996 me Sri Lanka ne jeeta. 1999, 2003, 2007 me Australia ne lagatar 3 World Cup jeete. Ricky Ponting ki team ko harana mushkil tha. 2011 me India ne apne ghar me World Cup jeeta. Sachin ka sapna pura hua. 2015 me Australia ne fir jeeta. 2019 me England ne New Zealand ko boundary count pe haraya, jo sabse controversial final tha. 2023 me Australia ne India ko final me haraya, jab pura India jeet ka jashn mana raha tha.

T20 World Cup ki alag kahani hai. 2007 me India ne Pakistan ko hara ke pehla T20 World Cup jeeta. Misbah ka wo scoop shot koi nahi bhoolega. 2024 me India ne fir se T20 World Cup jeeta Rohit Sharma ki kaptani me, South Africa ko hara ke. Virat Kohli ne final me 76 run banaye aur fir T20 se retirement le liya.

6. Duniya Ke Sabse Bade Players

Sachin Tendulkar - Cricket Ke Bhagwan

Sachin ko cricket ka bhagwan kehte hai. Unhone 100 century banayi hai, jo duniya me kisi ne nahi banayi. 200 Test aur 463 ODI khele. 34,000 se zyada run banaye. 16 saal ki umar me Pakistan ke khilaf debut kiya tha, jab Waqar Younis aur Wasim Akram jaise khatarnak bowler the. Sachin ne har bowler ko mara. Unki straight drive duniya ki sabse sundar shot mani jati hai. 2011 me World Cup jeetne ke baad unko pure team ne kandhe pe uthaya tha. Unka kehna tha - Log mujhe bhagwan kehte hai, par main sirf cricket khelna chahta tha.

Virat Kohli - King Kohli

Virat Kohli aaj ke zamane ke sabse bade batsman hai. Unko King Kohli kehte hai. Unhone 80 se zyada century banayi hai. Chasing me unse bada koi batsman nahi hai. Jab India ko 300 run chahiye hote hai, toh Kohli akela hi jeeta deta hai. Unki fitness sabse best hai. Wo roz 3 ghante gym karte hai. 2018 me unhone ek saal me 11 century banayi thi. Kohli ka cover drive dekhne ke liye log ticket lete hai.

MS Dhoni - Captain Cool

Dhoni ko Captain Cool kehte hai. Wo kabhi gussa nahi hote. Unhone India ko 3 ICC trophy jitayi - T20 World Cup 2007, ODI World Cup 2011, Champions Trophy 2013. Ye duniya me kisi kaptan ne nahi kiya. Dhoni ka Helicopter shot aur unki stumping ki speed duniya me sabse tez hai. Wo 0.1 second me stumping kar dete hai. Dhoni Ranchi ke ek chhote se sheher se aaye the, pehle wo ticket collector the. Aaj unki kahani pe film ban gayi hai.

Rohit Sharma - Hitman

Rohit ko Hitman kehte hai kyuki wo chhakke bahut marte hai. Unhone ODI me 3 baar double century banayi hai, jo kisi ne nahi banayi. Ek match me 264 run banaye the, jo duniya ka sabse bada score hai. Rohit ki pull shot sabse khatarnak hai. 2024 me unki kaptani me India ne T20 World Cup jeeta.

Jasprit Bumrah - Duniya Ka Best Bowler

Bumrah duniya ke sabse khatarnak bowler hai. Unka action alag hai, isliye batsman ko gend samajh nahi aati. Unki Yorker pe duniya ka koi batsman nahi tikta. 2024 ke T20 World Cup me wo Player of the Tournament bane. Unki economy sirf 4 ki thi, jab sab 9-10 ki economy se run de rahe the.

7. Batting, Bowling Aur Fielding - Teen Kala

Batting me technique sabse zaroori hai. Aage badh ke khelna, peeche ja ke khelna, cut, pull, drive - har shot ki alag technique hai. Aaj kal batsman reverse sweep bhi marte hai. Bowling me bhi kai prakar hai - Fast Bowling, Swing Bowling, Spin Bowling. Swing me gend hawa me idhar udhar jati hai. Wasim Akram ko Swing ka Sultan kehte hai. Spin me gend pitch pe gir ke ghoomti hai. Shane Warne aur Anil Kumble duniya ke sabse bade spinner the.

Fielding aaj kal sabse zyada badal gayi hai. Pehle fielding pe koi dhyan nahi deta tha, par aaj Jonty Rhodes, Ravindra Jadeja jaise fielder match jeeta dete hai. Ek run bachana bhi ek run banane jaisa hai. Catch pakadna, direct hit marna, boundary pe chhakka rokna - ye sab fielding ka hissa hai. Aaj har team ka alag fielding coach hota hai.

Cricket me fitness ab sabse zaroori ho gayi hai. Pehle player mote bhi hote the, par aaj Yo-Yo test pass karna padta hai. Virat Kohli ne fitness ka culture laya. Ab har player ko roz gym aur running karni padti hai. Isiliye aaj ke player pehle se zyada tez aur zyada fit hai.

8. Gully Cricket Se Team India Tak Ka Safar

India me har bachcha gully cricket se shuru karta hai. Gully me niyam alag hote hai - Ek tip out, pehli gend try ball, gend gum toh batsman paise dega. Wahi se bachcha seekhta hai. Fir school, fir district, fir state, fir Ranji Trophy, fir IPL aur fir Team India. Ye safar bahut lamba hai. 1 lakh bachcho me se 1 hi Team India tak pahunchta hai. Isliye jo pahunchta hai, wo bahut mehnat karta hai.

Aaj kal har gaon me cricket academy hai. Bachche 5 saal ki umar se coaching lete hai. Unke maa baap bhi unko support karte hai. Sachin ne kaha tha - Cricket me safal hone ke liye 10,000 ghante ki practice chahiye. Matlab roz 4 ghante practice karo toh 7 saal lagenge. Isliye mehnat hi sab kuch hai.

9. Cricket Ka Bhavishya - Aage Kya Hoga?

Cricket ka bhavishya bahut ujjwal hai. 2028 ke Olympics me cricket fir se aayega. 100 saal baad cricket Olympics me aayega. USA me bhi cricket badh raha hai. 2024 ka T20 World Cup USA me bhi khela gaya. Waha ke log bhi cricket seekh rahe hai. Aane wale time me T10 cricket bhi aayega, jo sirf 90 minute me khatam hoga. Women cricket bhi bahut tezi se badh raha hai. Smriti Mandhana, Harmanpreet Kaur aaj star hai. WPL bhi IPL ki tarah bada ho raha hai.

Technology bhi badh rahi hai. Ab AI se pata chalta hai ki bowler kaha gend dalega. Smart bat se pata chalta hai ki shot kitni tez thi. Aane wale time me cricket aur bhi tez aur aur bhi manoranjak ho jayega. Par ek cheez kabhi nahi badlegi - India ka cricket ke liye pyaar. Wo hamesha aisa hi rahega.

10. Aakhri Baat - Tum Bhi Khel Sakte Ho

Agar tumko cricket pasand hai, toh sirf dekhne se kaam nahi chalega. Roz 1 ghanta khelna shuru karo. Batting, Bowling dono try karo. Haar se mat daro. Har bade player ne pehle bahut haar dekhi hai. Sachin bhi pehle 2 match me 0 pe out hue the. Par unhone haar nahi mani. Isliye tum bhi mehnat karo, ek din tum bhi Team India ke liye kheloge. Cricket tumko discipline, teamwork aur mehnat sikhaega. Ye teen cheeze zindagi me bhi kaam aayegi.
